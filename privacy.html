Privacy Policy

Media Studio · Effective 2026-10-19

Who we are

Media Studio is a private tool that the operator of the Blip & Friends channels runs on their own computer to publish the channels' own videos to YouTube, Instagram, Facebook, Threads, TikTok, X, Pinterest, Bluesky, Mastodon and LinkedIn, and to start new episodes from the operator's phone through the channel's own Telegram bot. It is not offered to the public and has no user accounts of its own.

Data controller: the private individual who runs the Blip & Friends channels, the Netherlands. Contact: highvibes101.101@gmail.com.

Data we access

Media Studio only signs in to the operator's own accounts, and only with the permissions below. It never accesses viewers' data, comments, followers, messages, analytics, email or contacts. The only messages it reads are the ones sent to the channel's own Telegram bot (see Telegram).

YouTube (Google)

  • Upload YouTube videos (youtube.upload): to upload the channel's own videos and set their title, description, tags, privacy, "made for kids" and AI-disclosure settings.
  • View your YouTube account (youtube.readonly): to look up the channel's most recent uploads, so that a video is never uploaded twice after an interrupted upload, and to read the view, like and comment counts of the channel's own videos so the operator can see which ones work.

YouTube API Services. Media Studio uses YouTube API Services. By using Media Studio to publish to YouTube, you agree to be bound by the YouTube Terms of Service. Google's handling of this data is described in the Google Privacy Policy. You can revoke Media Studio's access to your YouTube account at any time in your Google security settings; Media Studio then deletes its stored sign-in from the computer. Media Studio stores no YouTube data other than the link to each video it published.

Instagram and Facebook (Meta)

  • pages_show_list, pages_read_engagement and business_management: to find the Blip & Friends Facebook Page the operator selects, and the Instagram account linked to it.
  • instagram_basic and instagram_content_publish: to read the Instagram account's ID and username, publish its own Reels and photos, and check its recent posts so nothing is posted twice.
  • pages_manage_posts: to publish and schedule the Page's own Reels and photos and check its recent Reels for the same reason.
  • instagram_manage_comments: to add the operator's own first comment under a post the app just published, and to read that post's comments only to check the first comment is not added twice. No other comments are stored or answered.
  • instagram_manage_insights and read_insights: to read the view, like, comment, share and save counts of the app's own posts, so the operator can see which posts work. Only these totals are stored, on the operator's computer; no data about the people who watched is read.

Threads (Meta)

  • threads_basic and threads_content_publish: to read the Threads account's ID and username, publish its own posts, and check its recent posts so a post is never published twice.
  • threads_manage_replies: to add the operator's own first reply under a post the app just published. The app does not read or answer other people's replies.
  • threads_manage_insights: to read the view, like, reply, repost and quote counts of the app's own posts. Only these totals are stored, on the operator's computer.

TikTok

  • user.info.basic: the account's open ID, and through the posting API its username, the visibility options it allows and its comment, duet and stitch settings.
  • video.publish: to post the account's own videos and check whether a post finished processing.

X

  • users.read: the account's ID and username.
  • tweet.write and media.write: to upload and post the account's own videos.
  • tweet.read: to check the account's recent posts so a video is never posted twice.
  • offline.access: to stay signed in between posts without asking again.

Pinterest

  • user_accounts:read: the account's username.
  • boards:read and boards:write: to find the Blip & Friends board the videos are pinned to, or create it once if it doesn't exist.
  • pins:write: to upload the account's own videos and create Pins with them.
  • pins:read: to check the board's recent Pins so a video is never pinned twice, and to read the impression, save and click counts of the app's own Pins.

Bluesky

  • Media Studio signs in with an app password that the operator creates for it in Bluesky (never the account password). An app password can post and upload, but cannot change the account's password or settings or read direct messages.
  • It reads the account's handle and identifier, uploads and posts the account's own videos, photos and text (videos through Bluesky's video service), replies to its own posts with the post's first comment, and reads the account's own recent posts so nothing is posted twice.

Mastodon

  • Media Studio uses the access token of an application the operator creates on the channel's own Mastodon server, limited to the scopes below.
  • read:accounts: the account's ID and username.
  • write:media and write:statuses: to upload and post (or schedule) the account's own videos, photos and text, and reply to its own posts with the post's first comment.
  • read:statuses: to check the account's recent posts so nothing is posted twice, and to read the favourites, boosts and replies of its own posts.

LinkedIn

  • openid and profile: the member's LinkedIn ID and name, to know whose posts to create.
  • w_member_social: to upload and post the member's own videos, photos and text. Media Studio does not read the member's feed, connections, messages or post statistics.
  • The sign-in lasts 60 days; after that the operator connects again.

Telegram

  • The operator can connect a Telegram bot of their own (created with Telegram's @BotFather) to a channel, to start episodes from their phone. The bot token is stored like the other sign-ins (see below).
  • Received: the messages sent to that bot. Media Studio only acts on messages from private chats the operator paired with a one-time code shown on their computer: commands, episode ideas and episode scripts (as text or a file). Messages from chats that are not paired, and from groups, are discarded without being stored or logged. While a pairing code is valid (10 minutes), wrong codes are counted per chat in memory only, never written to disk; the counts are dropped within a minute after the code is used, replaced or expires, and when Media Studio stops.
  • Sent: only to the paired chats: the episode prompt, short status replies, the cover image and the rendered video of the channel's own episodes.
  • Kept: the IDs of the paired chats (and the series each one uses), only on the operator's computer. Unpairing a chat or disconnecting the bot deletes them. Scripts sent to the bot become part of the channel's own episodes.
  • Telegram processes these messages under its own privacy policy.

How we use it

Only to publish and check the channels' own videos, when the operator asks for it or at a time they scheduled, and, through the Telegram bot, to start, save and render new episodes from the operator's paired chats and send the prompt, the cover and the video back to those chats. Wrong pairing codes are counted, in memory only, to protect pairing. Besides videos, the operator can publish the channels' own photo posts (one or several photos) and text posts to Instagram, Facebook, Threads, X, Pinterest, Bluesky, Mastodon and LinkedIn, and export them for TikTok; these use the same permissions listed above and are handled the same way as videos. We do not use any of this data for advertising, profiling or analytics, and we do not use it to develop, improve or train artificial-intelligence or machine-learning models.

Where it is stored

  • Sign-ins (access and refresh tokens, the Bluesky app password and the Telegram bot token) and app credentials are stored only on the operator's computer, in the operating system's secure credential store (macOS Keychain or Windows DPAPI). They are never written to files, logs or source code.
  • Records of published posts (title, caption, platform post ID and link) are kept in a local database and in the operator's private source repository.
  • The IDs of paired Telegram chats are kept in a local file on the operator's computer, not in the source repository.
  • Media Studio has no servers or cloud storage of its own.

Channel link pages

Each channel can have a link page on this site (for example /blip-and-friends/) with the channel's name, its public profile links and links to posts that are already public. These pages set no cookies, run no scripts, load nothing from other sites and collect nothing. They are hosted on GitHub Pages; GitHub may keep standard server logs as described in the GitHub Privacy Statement.

Sharing

We do not sell or share account data with anyone. Besides the Telegram Bot API (for the channel's own bot, see Telegram), Media Studio only communicates with the platform each video is posted to: Google (YouTube Data API), Meta (Instagram and Facebook Graph API, Threads API), TikTok (Content Posting API), X (X API), Pinterest (Pinterest API), Bluesky (the account's AT Protocol server and Bluesky's video service), the channel's own Mastodon server and LinkedIn (Posts, Images and Videos APIs).

Threads only accepts videos and photos from a public link, and Instagram only accepts photos that way. For those posts, the file is therefore placed briefly on GitHub (a temporary release in the public media-studio-site repository) and deleted as soon as the platform has fetched it. Only the video or photo is shared this way, never account data.

The sign-in pages on this site (meta-callback.html, threads-callback.html, tiktok-callback.html, pinterest-callback.html, linkedin-callback.html) only pass the one-time sign-in code from the platform to Media Studio on the operator's computer. They store nothing and load no third-party scripts.

Media Studio's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

A sign-in (including the Telegram bot token) is kept until the operator disconnects that platform in Media Studio, which deletes it from the computer (for YouTube and X it is also revoked with the platform; for Bluesky the session is ended). Access can also be removed at any time on the platform:

  • Google: myaccount.google.com/permissions
  • Facebook and Instagram: Facebook Settings → Apps and websites
  • Threads: Threads Settings → Account → Website permissions
  • TikTok: TikTok app → Settings and privacy → Security → Apps and services
  • Pinterest: Settings → Security and logins → Apps (or Claimed accounts → Apps)
  • X: Settings → Security and account access → Apps and sessions → Connected apps
  • Bluesky: Settings → Privacy and security → App passwords → delete "Media Studio"
  • Mastodon: your server → Preferences → Development → delete the "Media Studio" application
  • LinkedIn: Settings → Data privacy → Permitted services → remove Media Studio
  • Telegram: in Media Studio, Settings → Telegram → Unpair or Disconnect (deletes the paired chats and the bot token from the computer); in Telegram, stop or block the bot, or delete it with @BotFather (/deletebot)

Post records are kept as the channels' publishing history and can be deleted on request.

Children

The Blip & Friends videos are made for children, but Media Studio does not collect any information from children or other viewers; its Telegram bot only acts on the operator's own paired chats. Viewing happens on each platform, under its own rules and privacy policy. On YouTube, these videos are marked "made for kids"; on TikTok, comments are turned off for them.
